Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before you buy.

Which software do I need?

After Effects projects need Adobe After Effects (2022 or newer recommended). Sound effects and music work in any editor: Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve, CapCut, Final Cut and more.

Can I use the files in client work?

Yes. You can use everything in personal and commercial client projects. You can't resell or redistribute the original files.

How do I receive my files?

Checkout is handled securely by Gumroad. Right after payment you get an instant download link by email.

Do you add new products?

Yes — new projects, sounds and AI packs drop every week.

What if I have a problem with a file?

Contact us by email or Instagram DM and we'll help you quickly.
