Privacy Policy

L'EDITING app · Last updated: 16 November 2026

L'EDITING is a private tool made and used by Youssef Azzouggarh to manage his own Instagram professional account (@azzouggarh.edit) and its linked Facebook Page. It is not offered to other businesses and nobody else can log in to it.

What the app does

  • Publishes Youssef's own posts, reels and stories to his own account and Page.
  • Reads the comments written under his own posts.
  • When a person comments a keyword that a post asks for (for example "CLAUD"), replies under that comment and sends that person one direct message with the link they asked for.

What data it handles

  • Your Instagram username and the text of the comment you wrote under one of his posts.
  • The messages you exchange with the account after you asked for a link.
  • Whether you follow the account, only when a post says the link is for followers.

The app does not read your profile details, your contacts, your other posts or anything outside your interaction with this account.

How the data is used

Only to answer you: one public reply and the direct message with the link you asked for, plus one optional follow-up message inside the 24 hours after your last message. A short log (username, keyword, time) is kept so that the same person does not get the same message twice.

Where it is kept and who sees it

The log is stored on Youssef's own computer. It is not sold, not shared with anyone, not used for advertising and not sent to any other service. Messages are sent through the official Meta APIs.

How long it is kept

Log entries are kept for as long as the post's automation is active and are removed when it is deleted, or sooner if you ask.

Delete your data

To have your entry removed, send a direct message to @azzouggarh.edit on Instagram with the words "delete my data". Your entry is deleted within 7 days and you get a confirmation in the same conversation.
